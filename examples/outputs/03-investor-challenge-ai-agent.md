# Golden Example 03: Investor Challenge + AI Agent Lens

> Fictional/anonymized example. This demonstrates investor challenge mode.

## Example prompt

```text
使用 $pm-product-office-hours 的投资人质询模式，并按 AI Agent 模板挑战我。
我们想做一个面向跨境电商卖家的运营 Agent，自动发现差评、生成处理方案、创建客服工单、提醒运营调整商品页。
请从市场、护城河、商业模式、团队优势和 90 天验证计划质询。
```

## Harsh investor summary

This is a useful workflow, but not yet obviously a venture-scale company. The current pitch sounds like an AI workflow wrapper around existing helpdesk, marketplace, and review-monitoring tools. To be investable, the founder must prove a narrow wedge where the Agent does something existing tools do not: closes the loop from review detection to operational change, with measurable revenue/reputation impact.

## Stronger founder answer

We are not building a generic customer-service chatbot. Our wedge is “negative review operations” for cross-border sellers with 50-500 SKUs. These teams lose revenue because review signals are scattered across marketplaces, support tickets, and product pages. Our Agent detects high-risk review patterns, creates human-approved actions, and tracks whether the seller fixed the listing, logistics note, or support script. The first proof is reducing time-to-action on harmful review clusters from days to hours.

## 12 likely investor questions

| Question | Weak answer risk | Stronger answer |
|---|---|---|
| Why is this a company, not a feature? | “AI Agent is powerful” | Start with negative-review operations across marketplaces, support, and listing changes; expand after proving closed-loop action |
| Who pays? | “Cross-border sellers” | Ops lead or founder of 50-500 SKU seller paying from customer support/reputation budget |
| What budget do you replace? | “Manual work” | VA/review monitoring/helpdesk workflow cost and lost revenue from unresolved review clusters |
| Why now? | “LLMs are better” | Marketplace complexity + LLM classification/action drafting + seller pressure on review-driven conversion |
| What is the first wedge? | “All operations” | Detect repeated negative review pattern and create human-approved action plan |
| What can the Agent do autonomously? | “Everything eventually” | MVP only recommends and creates draft tasks; no marketplace write action without approval |
| How do you evaluate quality? | “Users like it” | Accepted action rate, false positive rate, time-to-action, repeated issue reduction |
| What is the moat? | “Data” | Workflow history, seller-specific issue taxonomy, marketplace action playbooks, integrations over time |
| Why won't helpdesk tools copy this? | “We move faster” | They optimize tickets; wedge is marketplace review-to-ops action loop across listing/logistics/support |
| How do you get first 10 customers? | “Online marketing” | Founder-led outreach to seller communities, agencies, and operators managing review pain |
| What is the pricing? | “Subscription” | $99-$499/month by SKU/review volume, pilot success tied to reduced time-to-action |
| What proves this in 90 days? | “MVP launched” | 10 sellers, 1,000 reviews processed, 50 accepted actions, 30% lower time-to-action |

## Market wedge

Beachhead:

- Cross-border e-commerce sellers
- 50-500 active SKUs
- Review volume high enough to hurt conversion, but team too small for dedicated analyst workflow

Expansion:

1. Review cluster detection
2. Support script/task generation
3. Listing and FAQ improvement recommendations
4. Marketplace and helpdesk integrations
5. Broader operations Agent

## Business model

| Item | Hypothesis |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Founder, ops lead, or customer experience lead |
| User | Ops specialist / customer support lead |
| Pricing | Monthly subscription by SKU/review volume |
| Entry plan | 14-day pilot with manual onboarding |
| Expansion | More stores, marketplaces, team seats, action automation |

## Moat hypothesis

Weak moat today:

- LLM-based classification and drafting can be copied.
- Review monitoring exists.

Potential compounding advantages:

- Seller-specific issue taxonomy
- Historical action-to-outcome data
- Marketplace-specific playbooks
- Integrations into helpdesk/listing/task tools
- Trust from human-approved actions before automation

## AI Agent boundary

| Area | MVP decision |
|---|---|
| Autonomy level | Copilot, not fully autonomous |
| Human approval | Required for all external actions |
| Write access | No direct marketplace edits in MVP |
| Failure cost | Medium: wrong suggestion wastes ops time or harms customer messaging |
| Evaluation | Accepted action rate, false positive rate, time-to-action |

## 90-day proof plan

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Week 1-2 | Interview 20 sellers; collect anonymized review workflows |
| Week 3-4 | Manual concierge analysis for 5 sellers |
| Week 5-6 | Prototype review cluster detection and task drafts |
| Week 7-10 | 10 seller pilots, weekly usage |
| Week 11-12 | Measure time-to-action reduction and paid conversion |

Success threshold:

- 10 sellers onboarded
- 1,000+ reviews processed
- 50+ human-accepted actions
- 30% reduction in time-to-action
- 3 paid conversions or signed pilot agreements

## Do-not-say list

Do not say:

- “我们是跨境电商 AI Agent 平台”
- “未来可以自动化所有运营”
- “市场很大，卖家很多”
- “大模型能力提升所以现在能做”

Say instead:

- “我们先解决跨境卖家差评到运营动作的闭环。”
- “MVP 不自动改店铺，只做检测、建议和人工确认。”
- “90 天内用 time-to-action 和 accepted action rate 证明价值。”
